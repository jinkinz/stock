② Capital & universe
In the ~2½ hours before the bell, rank the universe by pre-market gap and pre-market volume and scan those instead of yesterday's turnover leaders. Falls back automatically when there is no pre-market session or nothing has traded.
③ Strategy & risk
Set $/hour and it auto-calculates the session target. Or set Session Target directly and leave $/hour at 0.
Guaranteed mechanical rules, independent of AI judgment: Lock Profit sells a position once it gains that %; Stop Loss sells once it loses that % from entry; Trailing Stop sells a profitable position falling that % below its peak.
These free a slot on something other than price, so a position that is going nowhere stops blocking better setups. They are not free — the freed slot gets refilled and the replacement pays a full round trip. All but the time stop ship OFF because they measured worse than price-only exits over 60 replayed trades (breakeven −0.81 vs −0.45 expectancy; thesis break −4.88). Measure on your own window before enabling: python3 replay.py --legacy-exits to see the baseline.
Sizes each trade so a stop-out costs the same % of equity whatever the symbol's volatility, and sets each position's stop from its own ATR rather than a flat %. Turning sizing off reverts to the flat Max Trade Value (larger positions, larger swings); ATR stops still apply.
Requires trend, VWAP, volume, structure and momentum to agree before buying, instead of trusting one blended score. Fewer trades means less fee drag — and in testing the "almost agreed" band (4 of 5) was the worst-performing cohort.
Saves everything above — capital, horizon, strategy and risk — in one go.
